M unicipal heat vulnerability is no longer an abstract climate forecast; it is an active zoning catastrophe. As industrial land consolidation accelerates along our peripheral development corridors, ancient neem and tamarind canopies are cleared overnight to pour unshaded asphalt logistics bays and unventilated corrugated steel factories.

Between June and September, radiometric satellite readings documented a steady accumulation of latent thermal energy across these zones. Because asphalt absorbs roughly 95% of incoming solar radiation, nighttime dissipation cycles have completely collapsed, trapping lower-income neighborhoods in a perpetual convective greenhouse.

Empirical Field Findings: 6°C Surface Temperature Spikes

Our team deployed FLIR handheld radiometers across 40 continuous checkpoints along the border between the Udyog Kendra industrial perimeter and the dense resettlement colonies of Anand Nagar. The differential was staggering: within 12 meters of leaving shaded street trees, ambient ground temperatures shot upward.
